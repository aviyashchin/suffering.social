Interpretation

Read the total as a sensitivity test

$3.2T modeled burden

The value is useful for comparing assumption sets. It is not a forecast, a clinical estimate, or proof that social media caused every represented outcome.

Method & sources

A transparent model, designed to be disputed

The calculator combines mortality valuation, health-adjusted life years, healthcare expenditure, and productivity loss. Each term stays editable because uncertainty compounds.

Limitations

What this estimate cannot tell us

  • Published associations do not identify a single causal effect for every person or platform.
  • The counterfactual—what would have happened without social media—is not directly observable.
  • Costs overlap, vary across populations, and should not be interpreted as a clinical diagnosis.

Adolescent mental health · United States

What changed for teenagers around 2012?

A sharp mental-health inflection appears in national data around 2011 and 2012—at roughly the same moment smartphones and social platforms became fixtures of adolescence.

The timing is evidence, not proof. Correlation cannot tell us what would have happened in the counterfactual world without social media. A causal claim requires stronger designs and explicit assumptions.

The observed pattern

The slope changes before the debate catches up.

U.S. survey series show adolescent distress rising after a comparatively stable period. Different measures and datasets place the turn near 2011–2012; none alone isolates a cause.

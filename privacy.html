Privacy

This is a public research site about social media and mental health. It has no account system, lead form, newsletter signup, or advertising. It does use a visitor identification service, described below.

What may be measured

When enabled, the site records page views and a small set of explicitly marked link clicks. Event data is limited to the page path, deployment environment, site identifier, CTA identifier, and destination hostname. Query strings, calculator values, page text, and free-form input are excluded.

Service providers

  • Google Analytics 4 provides aggregate traffic measurement. Google advertising signals and ad personalization are disabled.
  • PostHog provides aggregate product analytics. Autocapture, session replay, console capture, exception capture, performance capture, and anonymous profiles are disabled.
  • Google Tag Manager may manage approved measurement configuration. Advertising and identity consent are denied for this site.
  • Sentry reports browser errors. Default PII collection and replay are disabled, and URLs and error context are scrubbed before sending.
  • Lemlist performs visitor identification. It attempts to identify the company, and where possible the individual, behind a visit, and makes that information available to us for business outreach. This is person-level identification rather than aggregate measurement, and it runs on every page of this site.

Your controls

Browser privacy tools and content blockers may block these services. Visitor identification is not consent-gated and does not respond to Global Privacy Control. To ask about access or deletion requests, or to ask us to delete an identification record, email privacy@subconscious.ai.

What we do not use

This site does not use RB2B, Vector, Leadsy, or session replay. It does not sell or share visitor data for cross-context behavioral advertising.

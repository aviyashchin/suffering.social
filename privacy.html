Privacy

This is a public research site about social media and mental health. It has no account system, lead form, newsletter signup, advertising, or person-level identification.

What may be measured

When enabled, the site records page views and a small set of explicitly marked link clicks. Event data is limited to the page path, deployment environment, site identifier, CTA identifier, and destination hostname. Query strings, calculator values, page text, and free-form input are excluded.

Service providers

If enabled, these providers may be used for the limited purposes below. They are disabled unless deliberately configured for this deployment.

  • Google Analytics 4 may provide aggregate traffic measurement. Google advertising signals and ad personalization are disabled.
  • PostHog may provide aggregate product analytics. Autocapture, session replay, console capture, exception capture, performance capture, and anonymous profiles are disabled.
  • Google Tag Manager may manage approved measurement configuration. Advertising and identity consent are denied for this site.
  • Sentry may report browser errors. Default PII collection and replay are disabled, and URLs and error context are scrubbed before sending.

Your controls

Browser privacy tools and content blockers may block these services. Global Privacy Control is honored for advertising and identity uses; those uses are disabled on this site regardless. To ask about access or deletion requests, email privacy@subconscious.ai.

What we do not use

This site does not use RB2B, Vector, Leadsy, session replay, or similar person-level identification services. It does not sell or share visitor data for cross-context behavioral advertising.

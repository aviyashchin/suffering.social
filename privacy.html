Privacy

This is a public research site about social media and mental health. It has no account system or advertising. Site measurement is aggregate-only.

Research update emails

If you choose to join the research update list, we save the email address you enter in our contact system. We use it only to contact you about major research or calculator updates. Your email is not sent to site analytics, error reports, or advertising systems. To be removed, email privacy@subconscious.ai.

What may be measured

When enabled, the site records one page view and a small set of explicitly marked research interactions. Event data is limited to the canonical host, canonical page path and location, deployment environment, site identifier, an approved interaction identifier, and the external referrer’s HTTPS origin when the browser supplies it. Query strings, URL hashes, referrer paths, calculator values, scenario state, page text, destinations, and free-form input are excluded.

Service providers

  • Google Tag Manager is the only Google script loader. It may manage Google Analytics 4 for aggregate traffic measurement. Analytics storage, advertising storage, ad user data, and ad personalization default to denied; Google Signals, User-ID, advertising features, and cross-domain measurement remain off.
  • Sentry may report browser errors when a release is configured. Default personal-information collection, tracing, and replay are disabled. Before an event is sent, user data, query strings, cookies, headers, request bodies, tags, custom context, calculator state, and non-navigation breadcrumbs are removed.

Your controls

Browser privacy tools and content blockers may block these services. To ask about this policy or a privacy request, email privacy@subconscious.ai.

What we do not use

This site does not use person-level identification, session replay, product profiling, or behavioral advertising. Provider rules exclude this hostname from portfolio-level identification and replay tags.
